# Scoring rubric (1–5 each, total out of 15)

## Business impact
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Directly moves revenue or cost by a large, measurable amount; affects many users |
| 4 | Clear measurable gain for one department |
| 3 | Useful productivity gain, harder to measure |
| 2 | Nice to have |
| 1 | Marginal |

## Feasibility
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Proven pattern; can demo in 2 weeks with off-the-shelf models |
| 4 | Proven pattern; needs integration work (4–8 weeks) |
| 3 | Needs custom pipeline or fine-tuning |
| 2 | Research-heavy or depends on uncertain model capability |
| 1 | Not realistic today |

## Data readiness
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Data is digital, structured and accessible via API |
| 4 | Digital but scattered across systems |
| 3 | Mostly documents/PDFs; needs extraction |
| 2 | Partly paper or locked in legacy systems |
| 1 | Data does not exist yet |
